# Argus closeout checklist

- Dedicated non-overlapping workdir/project is identified; install/binary path is resolved.
- `argus doctor` passed; `--deep` and any operationally required internal model/provider verification were used deliberately rather than on every routine check.
- Inputs/objective/authority are bounded; outputs and acceptance checks are named.
- Host adapter capability assumptions were verified; fallbacks are stated where conditional.
- Status was checked at dispatch, meaningful monitoring boundaries, after blocked/paused/replan, after every answer, and at closeout.
- No `waiting on you`, non-empty `pending_question`, or pending operator-decision card remains.
- Every intervention was answered within delegated authority or escalated with exact question, reason/options, and material evidence.
- Every answer was verified: `resolved: true` when returned, old marker cleared, typed card resolved when present, and continuation resumed/completed—or stop was durably recorded.
- No pending/running/paused item is hidden behind success-looking prose.
- Required Reviewer verdict is `done`.
- Requested artifacts exist and decisive checks support material claims.
- Material citations resolve and support each claim; current claims use fresh sources.
- Mission completion is not misreported as project/stage completion.
- Invoking surface receives compact outcome, paths, evidence, exceptions, and unresolved operator blockers—not private trajectory/logs.
